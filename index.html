FAQ

Common questions

Everything you might want to know before booking. Can't find your answer? Just give me a call.

How much does a visit cost?
A quick fix (up to 30 minutes) is $55, a standard visit (up to 1 hour) is $85, and an extended session (up to 2 hours) is $120. There's no call-out fee for anyone in the Redlands. I'll always let you know upfront if I think a job will take longer.
Do you help younger people and businesses too, or just seniors?
Everyone! I help seniors, families, people working from home and small local businesses. Whether you want patient one-on-one coaching or a fast, no-fuss fix, you're in the right place.
Can you fix things remotely, or do you have to come out?
Often I can sort it remotely the same day — I securely connect to your screen while you watch, which is quick and cheaper. If it needs hands-on attention, I'll come to your door.
What areas do you cover?
Redland Bay, Cleveland, Victoria Point, Capalaba, Wellington Point, Thornlands and surrounding suburbs. Not sure if you're in range? Just give me a call.
Do I need to know anything technical before you arrive?
Not at all. Just describe the problem in your own words — that's all I need. I'll figure out the rest when I get there.
How quickly can you come out?
Most weeks I can get to you within a few days, and I'm available Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 4 PM. For urgent issues, call me directly and I'll do my best to fit you in sooner.
How do I pay?
Cash, bank transfer, PayID or tap-to-pay — whatever's easiest. Payment is taken after the visit, and you'll get a simple invoice by text or email.
